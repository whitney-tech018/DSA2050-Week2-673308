Objective

The objective of this practical was to use SQL and Python to inspect, validate, clean, and analyse retail data. The analysis involved checking data quality, loading CSV files into SQLite, performing SQL queries and JOINs, validating row counts and sales totals, and combining customer data with regional information to generate meaningful business insights.

Key Findings

1. The customers table contained a duplicate CustomerID (C004), which caused one order to be duplicated during the JOIN and increased total sales from 113,500 to 122,600.

2. The Corporate customer segment generated the highest total sales, with 46,800 from 4 orders.

3. Nairobi generated the highest regional sales, with total sales of 39,800, although sales alone cannot determine the overall performance of a region.
